# Commands

Generated by `python -m spikemaul.tools.build_docs`. Do not edit.

spikemaul 0.1.0

- `--book` the sqlite file to work on
- `--on` the day to work as of, as 2025-05-06
- `--version` Show the version and exit.

## bridge

Structures and their load ratings.

### bridge add

Put a structure in the book.

- `CODE`, required
- `NUMBER`, required
- `POST`, required
- `--kind` what sort of structure
- `--spans` how many spans
- `--length`, required length in feet
- `--normal`, required normal rating, Cooper E
- `--maximum`, required maximum rating, Cooper E
- `--built`, required the year it was built

### bridge list

List the structures on a subdivision, weakest rating marked.

- `CODE`, required

## crossing

Grade crossings and their inventory numbers.

### crossing add

Put a grade crossing in the book.

- `CODE`, required
- `NUMBER`, required
- `POST`, required
- `ROAD`, required
- `--inventory`, required six digits and a check letter
- `--surface` timber, rubber, concrete or asphalt
- `--warning` crossbucks, flashers or gates
- `--private` a private crossing

## cwr

### cwr destress

- `CODE`, required
- `SPAN`, required
- `--neutral`, required the rail temperature it was left at
- `--by`, required who did it
- `--method` heater, rollers or cut and pull

### cwr heat

Make the morning heat call for a stretch.

- `CODE`, required
- `SPAN`, required
- `--air`, required the forecast air temperature
- `--sky` clear, partly or overcast
- `--degrees` the curve, where the stretch is one
- `--issue` put the order on if one is called for
- `--authority` whose authority the order goes on

### cwr log

Print the destress log for a subdivision, oldest first.

- `CODE`, required

### cwr neutral
